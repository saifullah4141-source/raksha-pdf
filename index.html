Raksha PDF — Scanner & Editor

A complete PDF toolkit for Android. Scan, read, edit, sign and organise — with the work done on your own phone.

What it does

  • Scan documents with the camera, with automatic edge detection and page filters.
  • Read PDFs full screen, with Easy Read reflow and Read Aloud.
  • Edit real PDF text, add text, sign, stamp, redact and watermark pages.
  • Recognise text (OCR) in 76 languages, including Urdu, Hindi, Bangla, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi and Odia.
  • Organise — merge, split, compress, rotate and reorder pages.
  • Resize & compress images to an exact file size, such as 200 KB for an online form.
  • Back up to your own Google Drive, optionally and only when you ask.
Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning, OCR and editing all run on the device itself. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you turn on Google Drive backup yourself — and then it goes to your Drive, not to us.
