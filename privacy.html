Privacy Policy — Raksha PDF

Applies to the Android app Raksha PDF — Scanner, Editor (com.mustfa.rakshapdf). Last updated: 5 September 2026.

The short version. Your documents stay on your phone. Scanning, text recognition and editing all happen on the device. We run no server and keep no copy of anything you scan. The only time a document leaves your phone is when you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive, or share it yourself.

1. Who this is from

Raksha PDF is developed by Mustfa Saifullah. For any question about this policy or about your data, write to saifullah4141@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Scanned pages and PDF files you create, open or edit
  • Signatures, stamps and watermarks you make
  • Notebook notes, Khata book entries, ID cards and resumes
  • Text recognised by OCR
  • App settings, folders, favourites and the trash

Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app, so this data is not copied off the device by the system either.

3. What happens entirely offline

  • Document scanning and page detection
  • Text recognition (OCR) — Google ML Kit and Tesseract both run on the device
  • PDF editing, merging, splitting, compressing and page organising
  • The AI assistant, which runs a language model locally on your phone

The text on your documents is not sent anywhere to be read.

4. Google Drive backup — optional

The app can copy your documents to a folder named RakshaPDF in your own Google Drive. This is off by default and only runs when you sign in and ask for it.

  • The app requests only the drive.file permission. That means it can see and manage only the files it created itself. The rest of your Drive is invisible to the app.
  • Files go to your Google account. We have no access to them and keep no copy.
  • Signing out from inside the app removes the account and the upload record from your phone. To withdraw the permission completely, remove Raksha PDF at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. What the app downloads

A few features fetch files from the internet. These downloads carry no personal data — they are ordinary public file requests.

WhatFromWhen
OCR language packs (Tesseract) github.com / tesseract-ocr The first time you pick a language ML Kit cannot read
Translation models Google ML Kit The first time you translate into a language
Offline AI model github.com Only if you choose to download it

6. Advertising and purchases

Ads

Free users may see an occasional full-screen advert from Google AdMob. AdMob is Google's service and may use your device's advertising ID to choose which advert to show. We receive no personal information from it. Google's handling of that data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Premium subscribers see no adverts.

Subscriptions

Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — the app never sees or stores your card, UPI or bank details. All the app keeps is whether your subscription is currently active.

8. What we never collect

  • We run no analytics of our own and no server that receives your data.
  • We do not read, index or transmit the contents of your documents.
  • We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

9. Children

Raksha PDF is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices

  • Delete any document from inside the app; emptying the trash removes it for good.
  • Clear the app's cache and data from Android Settings to erase everything it holds.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data from your phone.
  • Withdraw Drive access at any time, as described in section 4.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
